    OUR MISSION

    What We Do

    At Elevate the Circuit, our mission is to empower students through the transformative benefits of speech and debate. We partner with public middle and high schools to establish thriving forensics programs through what we do:

    We Provide Resources

    Equipping students and educators with 100% free turnkey curriculum kits, topic briefs, judge rubrics, and starter materials.

    Provide Coaching

    Connecting chapters with experienced varsity mentors and alumni coaches to train debaters and teach speech delivery.

    Organize Tournaments

    Hosting accessible local and regional forensics competitions with certified judge pools, constructive feedback, and fee grants.

    Start Chapters

    Providing turnkey guides and direct coordinator support to help students and faculty advisors found active speech and debate clubs.

    Coordinate Multi-State Meetings

    Facilitating cross-regional assemblies and collaborative leadership sessions connecting debaters from across multiple states.

    Organize Practice Opportunities

    Structuring weekly practice debates, inter-school scrimmage pairings, and low-stakes sparring rounds to build real-time confidence.

    100% FREE CHAPTER PACKAGES

    Bring Speech & Debate to Your School.

    Whether you are a student leader hungry to compete or an educator ready to sponsor a team, Elevate the Circuit equips you with official syllabi, practice motions, judge mentors, and tournament opportunities.
