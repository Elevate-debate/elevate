    EVENTS CHEAT SHEET

    Which Events Fit Your School?

    Not sure where to begin? Here is a breakdown of the most popular speech and debate events supported across our national chapter network.

    DEBATE • 2V2 PARTNERS

    Public Forum (PF)

    Partner-based debate centered on timely domestic and foreign policy topics. Emphasizes conversational persuasiveness accessible to everyday citizen judges.

    • Team Size: 2 vs 2
    • Focus: Current events, logic, evidence
    • Great for: Novices & High Schoolers
    DEBATE • 1V1 VALUES

    Lincoln-Douglas (LD)

    One-on-one philosophical debate examining the ethical principles behind societal questions (e.g. justice, individual rights, utilitarianism).

    • Team Size: 1 vs 1
    • Focus: Moral philosophy, political theory
    • Great for: Independent analytical thinkers
    DEBATE • 2V2 IN-DEPTH RESEARCH

    Policy Debate (CX)

    A rigorous, year-long deep dive into a single national policy topic. Teams craft detailed affirmative proposals and defend against complex counter-plans and disadvantages.

    • Team Size: 2 vs 2
    • Focus: In-depth research, policy mechanics, speed flowing
    • Great for: Dedicated researchers & intensive debaters
    DEBATE • LEGISLATIVE SIMULATION

    Congressional Debate

    Students simulate the U.S. Congress, drafting bills and resolutions, delivering 3-minute speeches, and voting using parliamentary procedure.

    • Team Size: Large chamber (15-25 students)
    • Focus: Politics, diplomacy, rhetoric
    • Great for: History and civics enthusiasts
    DEBATE • MIDDLE SCHOOL FAVORITE

    SPAR Debate

    Spontaneous Argumentation (SPAR) gives debaters 2 minutes of prep to argue fun or accessible topics. Eliminates prep paralysis and builds immediate round confidence.

    • Team Size: 1 vs 1 or small teams
    • Focus: Quick thinking, humor, public speaking
    • Great for: 6th-8th graders & first club meetings
    SPEECH • PERSUASIVE ORATORY

    Original Oratory (OO)

    Students write and memorize an original 10-minute speech addressing a significant societal issue, combining personal insight, research, humor, and a call to action.

    • Format: 10-minute memorized speech
    • Focus: Personal voice, rhetoric, societal critique
    • Great for: Writers, passionate advocates & solo speakers
    SPEECH • CURRENT EVENTS & NEWS

    Extemporaneous Speaking

    Speakers draw three current event questions, choose one, and have 30 minutes of prep to craft a 7-minute speech citing reputable domestic and international news sources.

    • Format: 30 minutes prep, 7-minute speech
    • Focus: Domestic/Foreign policy, evidence synthesis
    • Great for: News junkies, policy minds & analytical thinkers
    SPEECH • QUICK-FIRE THINKING

    Impromptu Speaking

    Speakers receive a prompt (quote, word, or cartoon) and have 7 total minutes to divide between preparation and delivering an insightful, organized, and engaging speech.

    • Format: 7 minutes total (1-2m prep, 5-6m speech)
    • Focus: Mental agility, storytelling, structure on the fly
    • Great for: Quick thinkers & building stage comfort
    SPEECH • ACTING & DRAMATICS

    Interpretation Events

    Performers bring published literary pieces, plays, or poetry to life through characterization, vocal modulation, and emotional storytelling without props or costumes.

    • Events: Dramatic (DI), Humorous (HI), Duo, POI
    • Focus: Acting, character development, emotional range
    • Great for: Theater lovers, performers & expressive storytellers
